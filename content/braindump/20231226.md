弱者才会陷入回忆的漩涡。\
回忆不能使你变强。唯一的用处是，当你处于一个不幸的时期，它是逃脱和慰藉。\
适当地寻求慰藉没关系，但记住美好幻象的尽头是虚无。\
要尽一切方法爬出深渊 — 寻找任何出口，seek help, talk to people (therapist, friends, family), read books, know and learn from how others overcome it. Start doing things at present. Indulging in the past does not change You and your state at present. Only starting to do things right now can bring changes, will make you not just happier but also stronger.\
