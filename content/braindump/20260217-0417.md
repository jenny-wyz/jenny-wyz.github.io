The difference I observe in myself in different settings

Now that I'm back to Canada (home). I do notice there's a big mindset difference depending on my physical location.\
When I am in switzerland, Im never able to truly "relax" and I never truly feel "home". I lose the ability to enjoy the time by myself. I always kinda seek "validation", or also companionship, in hopes to find a sense of purpose and fill the chronic "void" I am feeling.\
The more lonely I feel (although I try hard to suppress it), the more desperately and awkwardly I want to grasp connections and relationships, but then the stronger I fear to be over-attached to unreliable people or to be stuck in toxic situations.\
As a result of all these, the more anxious and uneasy I am, the less chill and confidence I have, and the less attractive I am.\
It is basically a malicious cycle:(\
So I know that the more desperate I cling onto something, the less likely I can get the thing I want; the more relaxed I am (essentially the more I "let go"), the easier things come (or at least I would be happier and more at peace).\
Logically I know it, but I just couldn't help being like that in switzerland. I don't know how to fix:(
