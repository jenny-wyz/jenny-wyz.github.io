### Punishment

My mom often forced me to eat food that was “good for my body/health” but that I happened to dislike (to varying degrees) (I was quite a 挑食 kid). E.g., carrots, fish, 鱼腥草, … \
(Is that pretty normal, though?)

How do you mean “force”?

情景重现：（不仅限于以上 category）
- “吃还是不吃（/说/去/做/……）？I count to three. One… Two…”

What happens after she counts to three? I don't remember, or perhaps I rarely/never dared to wait until she got to three. Due to fear and due to logic.

It would be some form of punishment. Maybe hitting my palms, maybe something else; in any case, it would all come with very 粗鲁地推搡/拖拽。

罚站 was a common form of low-stakes (and nonphysical) punishment in my household. I would stand still facing a corner of a wall/room for an unspecified, indefinite amount of time, until she returned and interrogated me again (usually a few hours later): what I had done wrong, whether there was anything I wanted to say, or something along those lines. If my answer was unsatisfactory (again, rarely), I think I would continue 罚站ing until she came back and asked me again. When I gave a satisfactory answer, I would usually be let free.

My strategy/logic was: time efficiency. Do I choose to suffer quickly or to suffer slowly? Clearly the former. Being rational was the only solution/answer. Every time （不仅限于以上 categories）, my goal was to get this done with as soon as possible.

Still, I think what hurt me the most was the verbal stuff.

But it was also the most common, everyday occurrence. I got so used to it that I had perfected my defense mechanism: separating my soul/ego/whatever from my body and from the situation, so that I could watch the whole thing from above, as if I weren't me. I built a 木偶 who was myself in such situations, who said the words as needed (in that situation, and to get me out of it) and bore the words for me, so that afterwards my (true self's) mood, self-esteem, identity, and whatnot would remain separate and unaffected by what had happened during the situation.

Although it was so common that I forgot pretty much all the instances, a few particular moments stood out and stuck somewhere deep in my memory.

(Same with the physical stuff, actually. I only remember one or two instances somewhat clearly.)

又是一个深夜。
