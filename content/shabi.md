Welcome. You have arrived at the page that was expecting you, which is not the page you were expecting, which is fine, because expectations are a kind of furniture and this room has none.

## Facts, approximately

- The moon is a rumor started by the tide.
- Every spoon remembers being a fork and is quietly ashamed.
- On Tuesdays the number seven takes the afternoon off, and all the sevens on clocks are played by a very dedicated six.
- Somewhere a pigeon is holding a meeting about you. Attendance is poor.
- The door you tried three times has written you a letter. It is mostly vowels.

## A short play

**STAIRCASE:** I go up.\
**OTHER STAIRCASE:** I also go up.\
**STAIRCASE:** Then who goes down?\
**OTHER STAIRCASE:** (long pause) The cheese.

*Curtain. The curtain is also cheese.*

## Instructions

1. Think of a word.
2. Think of a different word.
3. Put them together.
4. Congratulations, you are now a compound.
5. The word you first thought of was "lamp." Everyone's is. We are all, in the end, lamp.

## Weather

Partly abstract, with a 40 percent chance of nouns. Winds from the direction of yesterday at speeds best described as "eventually."

## Exit

There is no exit. There is, however, a very good soup. It is in the next room, which is this room, which is why the soup is cold.

Come back in a day. The door may have forgiven you.
