Typical ensemble:

- Bass, drum, lead guitar, rhythm guitar, vocal
  - Metal: same but harder

### Jazz Rock

- Jazzy structure or instrument

### Blues Rock

### Hard Rock

- Distorted guitar; fancy guitar solo
- Raspy/ripped/radical vocals
- (inspired by blues rock)

Listen:

- Led Zeppelin: 2nd album
- Black Sabbath - Paranoid
- The Who - Baba O'Riley

### Psychedelic Rock

- Very distorted, reverberated guitar
- Integrates garage, blues rock

Listen:

- The Doors - Break On Through
- Jimi Hendrix - 1983... (A Merman I Should Turn to Be)

### Art Rock

- Integrates classical music (concepts)
- Rather complex harmony & structure
- Ambient, dreamy, slow
- Chill, lofi vocals

Listen:

- Radiohead - No Surprises
- King Crimson - I Talk to the Wind, Epitaph
- Pink Floyd - Wish You Were Here

### Progressive Rock

- More "artsy", some symphonic
- Unique performance arrangements
- Usually not as fast and intense
- (insp. by classical, jazz, literature)

Listen:

- Pink Floyd - Shine On You Crazy Diamond
- Genesis - Dancing with the Moonlit Knight

### Post Rock

- More chill, atmospheric
  - Integrates slowcore, ambient
- (orig. from alternative rock)

Listen:

- Talk Talk - Myrrhman
- Slint - Washer
- Sigur Rós - Ágætis byrjun

### Math Rock

- Complex, diverse rhythms
  - 数拍子困难
- Multi-rhythmic guitar, polyphonic bass
- (insp. by post-hardcore, noise rock)

Listen:

- Slint - Spiderland
- toe - Two Moons

### Post Hardcore

- Complex, constant guitar / drum flow
- Maybe raspy vocals
