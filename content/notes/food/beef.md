### Sirloin

- **Position on Cow:** Located in the hindquarter, just behind the short loin.
- **Identification:** Large, flat, and relatively lean cut. (外圈一层完整脂肪)
- **Texture:** Moderately tender with a balanced flavor.
- **Price Range:** Moderate.
- **Cooking Methods:** Grilling, broiling, roasting, and stir-frying.
- **Facts:** Divided into top sirloin (more tender) and bottom sirloin (less tender). Versatile and can be used in a variety of dishes.

### Rib Eye

- **Position on Cow:** Located in the rib section, spanning ribs 6 to 12.
- **Identification:** Marbled with fat, round, and often with a fat cap. (中间一块圆形脂肪)
- **Texture:** Very tender and juicy due to high marbling.
- **Price Range:** High.
- **Cooking Methods:** Grilling, pan-searing, and roasting.
- **Facts:** Known for its rich flavor and tenderness. Often served bone-in for added flavor.

### Short Loin (T-bone)

- **Position on Cow:** Located behind the ribs and in front of the sirloin.
- **Identification:** Contains both the strip steak and the tenderloin.
- **Texture:** Extremely tender, especially the tenderloin portion.
- **Price Range:** High.
- **Cooking Methods:** Grilling, broiling, and roasting.
- **Facts:** Home to some of the most prized cuts like T-bone and Porterhouse steaks.

### New Yorker (Strip Steak)

- **Position on Cow:** Part of the short loin, adjacent to the tenderloin.
- **Identification:** Long, rectangular steak with a strip of fat along one side.
- **Texture:** Tender and flavorful but less so than the rib eye.
- **Price Range:** High.
- **Cooking Methods:** Grilling, broiling, and pan-searing.
- **Facts:** Often referred to as a “strip steak” and is known for its balance of flavor and tenderness.

### Flat Iron

- **Position on Cow:** Cut from the shoulder (chuck), specifically the top blade.
- **Identification:** Rectangular, with uniform thickness, and noticeable marbling.
- **Texture:** Exceptionally tender, second only to the tenderloin.
- **Price Range:** Moderate.
- **Cooking Methods:** Grilling, pan-searing, and broiling.
- **Facts:** Gained popularity for its tenderness and affordability. Requires removal of a tough central membrane.

### Tenderloin (Filet Mignon)

- **Position on Cow:** Runs along the spine, in the short loin and sirloin.
- **Identification:** Small, cylindrical, very lean.
- **Texture:** The most tender cut with a very fine texture.
- **Price Range:** Very high.
- **Cooking Methods:** Grilling, broiling, pan-searing, and roasting.
- **Facts:** Often the most expensive cut, prized for its tenderness but has a milder flavor compared to more marbled cuts.

### Brisket

- **Position on Cow:** Located in the breast or lower chest.
- **Identification:** Large, flat cut with a distinct layer of fat.
- **Texture:** Tough but becomes tender with slow cooking.
- **Price Range:** Low to moderate.
- **Cooking Methods:** Slow cooking, smoking, braising.
- **Facts:** Popular in BBQ for its ability to become very tender and flavorful when cooked low and slow.

### Chuck

- **Position on Cow:** Located near the shoulder and neck area.
- **Identification:** Large, with significant marbling and connective tissue.
- **Texture:** Tough, needs long, slow cooking to become tender.
- **Price Range:** Low to moderate.
- **Cooking Methods:** Braising, stewing, slow cooking.
- **Facts:** Versatile cut often used for ground beef due to its rich flavor.

### Flank

- **Position on Cow:** Lower abdominal area, below the loin.
- **Identification:** Flat, long, and relatively lean.
- **Texture:** Coarse grain, can be tough if not cooked and sliced correctly.
- **Price Range:** Moderate.
- **Cooking Methods:** Marinating and grilling, stir-frying.
- **Facts:** Must be sliced against the grain to improve tenderness.

### Skirt

- **Position on Cow:** Diaphragm muscle, located in the plate section.
- **Identification:** Long, flat cut with visible muscle fibers.
- **Texture:** Flavorful but can be chewy if not cooked and sliced properly.
- **Price Range:** Moderate.
- **Cooking Methods:** Grilling, broiling, stir-frying.
- **Facts:** Popular for fajitas and stir-fries due to its intense beef flavor.

### Round

- **Position on Cow:** Hind leg.
- **Identification:** Large, lean, and fairly tough.
- **Texture:** Lean and slightly tough, best when cooked slowly.
- **Price Range:** Low to moderate.
- **Cooking Methods:** Roasting, braising, slow cooking.
- **Facts:** Often used for deli meats, ground beef, and roasts. Slicing thinly helps improve tenderness.

### Rump

- **Position on Cow:** Upper part of the hindquarters.
- **Identification:** Lean and firm.
- **Texture:** Can be slightly tough, best suited for slow cooking methods.
- **Price Range:** Moderate.
- **Cooking Methods:** Roasting, braising, grilling (when marinated).
- **Facts:** Known for its rich flavor and often used for roast beef.

### Shank

- **Position on Cow:** Lower part of the legs.
- **Identification:** Very lean, with a lot of connective tissue.
- **Texture:** Very tough, requires long cooking to become tender.
- **Price Range:** Low.
- **Cooking Methods:** Braising, slow cooking, soups, and stews.
- **Facts:** Used to make osso buco, a famous Italian dish. Great for adding flavor to soups and stews due to its rich marrow content.
