## Hydrating & moisturizing

| Ingredient | Problem | Effects | Side effects |
|---|---|---|---|
| Hyaluronic acid | Dryness and dehydration | Hydrates and plumps the skin | Rare, possible redness in some |
| Glycerin | Dry skin | Moisturizes and softens the skin | Generally well tolerated |
| Ceramides | Damaged skin barrier | Restores and strengthens the skin barrier | Minimal risk of irritation |
| Fatty acids (e.g. linoleic acid) | Damaged skin barrier | Nourishes and repairs the skin barrier | Generally well tolerated |
| Shea butter | Dry, rough skin | Moisturizes, softens and soothes | Rare allergies |

## Exfoliants

| Ingredient | Problem | Effects | Side effects |
|---|---|---|---|
| AHAs (e.g. glycolic acid) | Uneven skin tone, texture | Exfoliates, improves skin tone | Potential irritation |
| BHAs (e.g. salicylic acid) | Acne, clogged pores | Exfoliates, treats acne | Can cause dryness or redness |

## Anti-aging

| Ingredient | Problem | Effects | Side effects |
|---|---|---|---|
| Vitamin A, retinoids (e.g. retinol) | Fine lines, wrinkles | Promotes skin renewal, reduces signs of aging | May cause irritation or dryness |
| Peptides | Loss of skin elasticity | Supports collagen production | Rare risk of allergic reactions |
| Vitamin C (ascorbic acid) | Sun damage, pigmentation | Antioxidant, brightens skin | Irritation in high concentrations |
| Coenzyme Q10 (ubiquinone) | Fine lines, wrinkles | Antioxidant, supports collagen production | Generally safe, occasional irritation |

## Sun protection

| Ingredient | Problem | Effects | Side effects |
|---|---|---|---|
| Zinc oxide | Sun protection | Blocks UVA and UVB rays | Generally well tolerated |
| Titanium dioxide | Sun protection | Reflects and scatters UV rays | Minor irritation in some |
