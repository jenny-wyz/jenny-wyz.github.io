A sample note in the music subsection. Notes live in subfolders under `content/notes/` (music, tech, literature, philosophy, history, biology), and each note also carries a category tag so it shows up under `/categories/` across the whole site.

The Aria states a 32-bar bass line. Every third variation is a canon, at a widening interval: unison, second, third, up to the ninth. Variation 30 breaks the pattern with a quodlibet, and then the Aria returns unchanged, which sounds different only because the listener is.
