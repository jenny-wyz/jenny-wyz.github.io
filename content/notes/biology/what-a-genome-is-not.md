A sample biology note. The genome is often called a blueprint, a program, or a book. A blueprint maps one-to-one onto a structure; the genome does not. A program runs deterministically on fixed hardware; cells are neither. A book is read linearly; the genome is read in loops, in parallel, and differently in every cell type. Each metaphor is useful for exactly one lecture and misleading after that.
