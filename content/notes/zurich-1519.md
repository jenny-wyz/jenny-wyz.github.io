A sample history note. On 1 January 1519, Huldrych Zwingli began his post at the Grossmünster in Zurich and, instead of following the prescribed readings, announced he would preach through the Gospel of Matthew from the beginning. A small procedural choice that turned out to be the start of the Swiss Reformation.
