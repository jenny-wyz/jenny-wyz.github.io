## Ingredients

- 1 cup short-grain rice, rinsed
- 8 to 10 cups water (more for thinner congee)
- 1 teaspoon neutral oil
- Pinch of salt

## Method

1. Soak the rinsed rice in the oil and a little water for 30 minutes. This helps the grains break down.
2. Bring the water to a boil in a heavy pot, add the rice, and return to a boil.
3. Lower to a bare simmer, lid slightly ajar, and cook 60 to 90 minutes, stirring now and then so nothing sticks.
4. Salt at the end. Top with anything: century egg, pickles, scallions, white pepper, a drizzle of sesame oil.

## Notes

Leftover cooked rice also works; cut the time in half.
