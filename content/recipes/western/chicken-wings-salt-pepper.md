## Ingredients
- chicken wings - ~10
- Marination 
  - cooking wine - 1 tbsp
  - soy sauce - 1 tbsp
  - oyster sauce - 1 tbsp
  - salt - 1 tsp
  - pepper
  - (or pepper&salt powder)
  - (葱姜)
- egg - 1
- starch - 1 tbsp

## Procedure
1. squeeze blood bottom up
2. absorb moisture
3. poke poke poke
4. marinate 30 mins
5. coat w egg and starch
6. hot oil in pan, place wings
7. low heat, 2 mins each side first
8. flip sides every min for 10 mins
9. high heat 1 min at the end
10. sprinkle s&p/cumin powder
