## Ingredients
- chicken wings - ~13
- marination:
  - salt - 1g
  - pepper - 1g
  - soy sauce - 10g
  - oyster sauce - 8g
  - oil - 20g
- honey - 25g

## Procedure
1. absorb chicken moisture w paper towel
2. marinate in a bowl
3. cover w lid/wrap, marinate in fridge overnight
4. place wings on alu foil in tray, place alu foil on top
5. preheat over to 150 deg, bake 1hr
6. mix honey (and 3ml water) to liquidy texture
7. coat 1 side of wings w honey
8. place on top shelf of oven, broil in low heat 3mins
9. flip wings, coat w honey, broil 3 mins
10. (sprinkle white sesame for decoration)
