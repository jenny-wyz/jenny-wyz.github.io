## Ingredients 
- Kürbis  ~500g
- Milch  ~200g
- Käse  1-3 Scheiben
- 水淀粉/水糯米粉
- Zucker  0-5 tsp

## Procedure
1. Steam seedless pumpkin til soft, peel skin
2. Milk & cheese in pot, low heat, stir til cheese melts
3. Pumpkin & milk in blender
4. Pour back into pot, low heat
5. Add 水淀粉 & sugar, stir
6. Serve
