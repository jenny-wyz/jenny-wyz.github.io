## Ingredients
- Spices
  - sugar - 1 tbsp
  - salt - 1 tsp
  - garlic powder - 1 tbsp
  - onion powder - 1 tbsp
  - white pepper powder - 1 tbsp
  - paprika powder - 1 tbsp
  - cayenne pepper powder - 1 tsp
  - turmeric powder - 1 tsp
  - chicken powder - 1 tbsp
- Chicken wings - 12-18
- Baking powder - 1 tsp
- Honey glaze:
  - honey - 2 tbsp
  - oil - 1 tbsp

## Procedure
1. absorb chicken moisture
2. mix spices in bowl
3. spread spices over chicken wings, rub 2 mins
4. (put wings in bowl 2, add 2 tbsp water to bowl 1, pour spiced water into bowl 2)
5. cover with lid, marinate in fridge overnight
6. add 1 spoon baking powder, mix
7. refrigerate 30-60 mins
8. add some oil, mix
9. place wings on oven rack, place tray w alu foil underneath
10. preheated oven to 210 deg, roast for 20 mins
11. honey glaze: mix honey and oil at 2:1
12. brush 1 side of wings w honey glaze
13. roast 2 mins
14. brush the other side w honey glaze
15. roast 2 mins
