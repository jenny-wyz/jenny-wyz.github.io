## Ingredients
- Arborio rice - 1 cup
  - do not wash
- white wine - 0.5 cup
  - or rice wine
- chicken stock - 3-4 cups
  - or: water + chicken boullion
- onions - 1 big / 2 small
- garlic - 2 cloves
- butter
- salt, pepper, (thyme)

## Procedure
1. warm stock
2. dice onion, mince garlic
3. pan, medium heat, butter melts
4. onion in, golden, garlic in
5. rice in, roast 2 min; keep stirring from now
6. wine in, 1 min
7. one scoop at a time, stock in; repeat 15-30 min, til al dente
8. heat off; salt, pepper, butter in, mix
9. serve
