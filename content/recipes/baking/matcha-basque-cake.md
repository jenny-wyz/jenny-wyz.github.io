## Ingredients 
- Frischkäse - 350g
  - 300g ok
- Vollrahm - 160g
- Eier - 3
  - 2 whole, 1 yolk
- Maisstärke - 10g
- Zucker - 80g
  - sweet tooth: 100g
- Matchapulver - 20g
  - heiss Wasser (70C) - 50g

## Procedure
1. Preheat oven to 240C
2. Soften cream cheese: microwave 200w (low), 2min
3. Big bowl: mix cream cheese, sugar, starch
4. Med bowl: matcha powder + hot water, whisk
5. Warm cream: microwave 500w (med), 20s
6. Med bowl: in 2 batches, add cream, mix well
7. Big bowl: add eggs once at a time, mix; add matcha cream, mix
8. Sift mixture into 6’’ mold w parchment paper
9. Bake: 230C, 24min
10. Cool to room temp, then refrigerate for min 1h
11. Devourrrr!
