## Ingredients
- egg yolk - 6
- sugar - 60g
- mascarpone - 500g
- egg white
- (milk - add as needed, 2-3 spoons)
- (vanilla extract / lemon)
- (cream)
- savoiardi - ~20
- espresso - 1/3 cup
- cocoa powder

## Procedure
1. Separate egg yolk & white
2. Mix yolk + sugar til creamy
3. Add little vanilla / some lemon juice
4. Add mascarpone (& cream), mix til desired consistency
5. beat egg white, sugar, gradually, add to yolk mixture
6. Dip each savoiardi in espresso for 1-2s
7. Layer savoiardi and custard mixture in container
8. Sift cocoa powder on top
9. Refrigerate for >2h
