## Ingredients
- Kurbis - 350g
- Frischkäse - 200g
- Zucker - 60g
- Vollrahm - 80g
- Eier - 3
- Maisstärke - 15g

## Procedure
1. Big bowl: room temp cream cheese + sugar, mix
2. Steam peeled pumpkin til soft
3. In mixer: steamed pumpkin, cornstarch, cream
4. Add pumpkin mixture to bowl, mix
5. Add eggs 1 by 1, mix
6. Sift into mold lined w parchment paper
7. oven: 230C, 22 min
