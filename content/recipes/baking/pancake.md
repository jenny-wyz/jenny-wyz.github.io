## Ingredients
- flour - 150g
- sugar - 1.5 tbsp / 12g
- baking powder - 8g
- salt - 1g
- milk - 250g
- unsalted butter - 20g
- egg - 1
- (vanilla extract)

## Procedure
1. melt butter
2. Bowl: milk + flour, mix/whisk
3. add sugar + salt + melted butter + egg, mix
4. add baking powder, mix
5. medium heat, thin oil in pan, 微冒烟
6. big spoon of mixture to pan, bubbles, turn side, 30 more sec
7. done, serve with syrup +/ butter
