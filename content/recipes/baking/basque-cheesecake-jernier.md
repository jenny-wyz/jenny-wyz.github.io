## Ingredients
- Frischkäse - 300g
- Vollrahm - 150g
- Eier - 2
- Zucker - 60g
- Maisstärke - 10g

## Procedure
1. Big bowl: room temp cream cheese + sugar, mix
2. Add eggs 1 by 1, mix
3. Heat cream to 50C, 分次 add, mix
4. Add cornstarch
5. Sift into mold lined w parchment paper
6. oven: 230C, 24 min
