## Ingredients 
- Eggs - 3
- Low gluten flour - 25g
  - = 20g flour + 5g corn starch (4:1)
- White sugar - 100g
- Greek yogurt (ohne zucker) - 500g 

## Procedure
1. Mix eggs + sugar
2. Add sifted flour, mix
3. Add yogurt, mix
4. Crumple baking paper, place into mold (~6 inch)
5. Pour mixture into mold, tap to remove air
6. Oven: 170 degrees, 35min  /  230, 23
7. Chill 3h in fridge 
8. Devour
