## Dough

## Ingredients 
- All purpose flour - 360g
- Salt - 2.5g
- Butter - 274g
- Ice water - 90g

## Procedure 
1. Cut butter into small pieces
2. Mix flour + salt
3. Add butter, mix well til 松散
4. Gradually add water while mixing
5. Gently, lightly fold and press dough
6. Plastic wrap around, refrigerate overnight

## Filling 

## Ingredients 
- Apples (sour) - 1100g (orig 1350)
- Sugar - 70g (orig 175)
- Cinnamon powder - 1g
- Honey - 60g (orig 140)
- Corn starch iced water - 适量

## Procedure 
1. Peel apples, cut to bite slices
2. Add sugar, cinnamon powder, honey
3. Mix, marinate 5min
4. Separately, mix ice water + corn starch
5. Low heat, boil apple til soft, juice gone
6. Add corn water gradually 
7. Apple filling 出锅 chill

## Baking

## Ingredients 
- Egg - 1
- Salt - 0.5 spoon
- Sugar - 适量
- Baking stones

## Procedure 
1. Press dough, fold a few times, 擀平
2. Cut a circular dough, place in plate
3. 戳些孔，盖锡纸，铺重石
4. Oven: 185 degrees, 30min
5. Out, 晾凉
6. Mix 1 egg + 0.5 spoon salt
7. Place cooled filling on dough 
8. 边缘刷蛋黄液
9. Other dough 擀平，铺上，粘住边缘
10. Cut out excess
11. 边缘捏花纹
12. 上面刷蛋液
13. 扎些气孔
14. 撒糖粒
15. Oven: 185 degrees, 35min
