## Ingredients
- Frischkäse - 300g
- Vollrahm - 167g
- Eier - 2
- Zucker - 60g
- Salz - 0.7g
  - or none. migros frischkäse is kinda salty already.
- 70% Schokolade - 120g
- Cocoapulver - 6g

## Procedure
1. Big bowl: room temp cream cheese + sugar, mix
2. Sift cocoa powder into bowl, + salt, mix
3. Add eggs 1 by 1, mix
4. Melt chocolate, add to bowl, mix
5. Heat cream to 50C, 分次 add, mix
6. Sift into mold lined w parchment paper
7. oven: 230C, 23 min
