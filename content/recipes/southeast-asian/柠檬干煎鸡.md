## Ingredients
- Marination
  - soy sauce
  - oyster sauce
  - 鱼露
  - salt
  - 葱姜
  - lemon juice & zest
- lemon - 4 slices
- basil
- onion
- garlic

## Procedure
- marinate 30 mins
- cook chicken
- cook onion, garlic
- add chicken back in
- add lemon, basil
- (squeeze lemon juice)
